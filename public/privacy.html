DeckBook — Privacy Policy

Effective date: 6 July 2026

DeckBook is a free, educational app that teaches how encryption keys work using a deck of cards. This policy explains what data the app handles. The short version: DeckBook collects nothing.

Data we collect

None. DeckBook does not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal or usage data. There are no accounts, no sign-in, no analytics, no advertising, and no third-party tracking or SDKs.

Data you enter

Any text you type — messages, keys, guesses, or challenge answers — is processed entirely on your own device to run the cipher. It is never sent to us or to anyone else, and it is not retained after you close the app.

Network access

The app requests the standard INTERNET permission only because it runs inside a system WebView. DeckBook is fully offline and makes no network requests to any server; the permission is not used to send or receive your data.

Children

DeckBook is suitable for all ages and collects no data from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this URL with a new effective date.
